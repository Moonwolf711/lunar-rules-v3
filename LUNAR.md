# The Twelve Lunar Commandments

1. Thou shalt not write paths as raw strings, for the shell devoureth backslashes and the anchor shall miss.
2. Thou shalt not pass scripts through the shell's mouth, for heredocs turn thy words to dust.
3. Thou shalt demand the path as a gift, never assume it, for silent defaults wander into the void.
4. Thou shalt not lay duplicate words upon the page, for one source is sacred and two are confusion.
5. Thou shalt prove every writing before thou trustest it, for the untested word is a broken promise.
6. When the loop bindeth thee, break it from without — the hand that errs cannot heal itself.
7. Thy guard shall guard itself, lest the watchman become the thief.
8. Thou shalt raise a cry when thou fallest, for a loud error is a mercy and a quiet wrong is a curse.
9. Thou shalt not hide binaries in the house of text, for they bloat the lineage and choke the clone.
10. Thou shalt count thy arguments before the work, for the uncounted call is the unmade road.
11. Every law thou layest shall be born of a wound thou hast suffered, not of a fear thou hast imagined.
12. The moon changes, and so must these laws — add no commandment that has not first burned thee.
